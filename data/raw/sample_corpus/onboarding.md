# Helix — New Hire Onboarding

Welcome to Helix. This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Day 1

You will receive a MacBook Pro pre-imaged with the Helix engineering profile. Sign in with the temporary password emailed to your personal address; you will be prompted to set a new password and enroll a YubiKey.

By end of day 1, you should have:

- Logged into Okta and enrolled MFA
- Cloned `helix-core`, `helix-control`, and `helix-console`
- Joined the `#general`, `#engineering`, and your team's Slack channel
- Met your assigned onboarding buddy

## Week 1

In your first week, complete the following training modules in Workday:

- Helix Code of Conduct
- Data Handling 101 (mandatory; SOC 2 requirement)
- Incident Response 101 (mandatory; PagerDuty primer)
- Engineering Standards (recommended)

Pair with your onboarding buddy on at least one shipped pull request. Your first PR is traditionally a typo fix or doc change to get familiar with the review process.

## Vacation Policy

Helix offers unlimited PTO. There is no formal accrual or cap. Engineers are expected to take at least 15 days off per calendar year. Vacation requests longer than 5 consecutive business days require manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. There is no cap; we trust you. If you are sick for more than 3 consecutive days, please notify your manager and HR.

## Parental Leave

All new parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave, regardless of gender or relationship to the child. An additional 4 weeks of part-time return-to-work transition is available in the 6 months following primary leave.

## Equipment

Standard issue is a MacBook Pro 16" with M3 Max, 36GB RAM, 1TB SSD. Engineers may request a Linux workstation in lieu of a Mac with manager approval.

Headphones up to $400 are reimbursed. Standing desks and ergonomic chairs are provided in offices; remote employees receive a one-time $1,500 home office stipend.

## Health Insurance

Helix sponsors three medical plans through Anthem: Bronze, Silver, and Gold. The company covers 100% of the Silver plan premium for the employee and 80% for dependents. Open enrollment is in November each year.

Vision and dental are bundled and fully employer-paid for employee + dependents.

## 401(k)

Helix matches 100% of contributions up to 6% of salary, vesting immediately. Enrollment is automatic at 4% pre-tax after 30 days. You may change contribution levels in Workday at any time.
